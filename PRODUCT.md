# Product

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
RocketHacks organizers and head judges run day-of judging: import Devpost projects, assign judges, review scorecards, and decide winners. Judges score tables on phones under time pressure. Participants apply and check in through the same site, but the judging portal is an organizer tool, not a marketing surface.

Primary task on any judging admin screen: find the truth about a project or a judge's load without drowning in sheets.

## Brand & Personality
Direct, operational, calm under pressure. Dark admin shell already in use (`#030c1b` / `#0a1628`, blue actions, yellow accents). Prefer clarity over decoration.

## Anti-references
SaaS marketing dashboards, purple glow kits, dense card grids that hide the decision, forcing organizers to open Excel to understand a close race.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast on body text. Keyboard-reachable expand/collapse. `prefers-reduced-motion` for any motion. Tables scroll horizontally on small screens rather than truncating scores.
